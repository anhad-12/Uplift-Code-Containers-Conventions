WHAT WE ARE BUILDING
Uplift tells you what a code change will break, PROVES it with a failing test, and REPAIRS it
with sandboxed IBM Bob workers, then shows the real numbers on a dashboard.
Predict -> Prove -> Repair -> Verify. Two modes: Impact (a patch) and Migrate (a dependency upgrade).
Demo target: a small FastAPI shop. Scenarios: S1 get_user returns None, S2 dollars->cents, S3 Pydantic v1->v2.
